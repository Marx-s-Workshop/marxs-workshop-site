Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5/11/2026

Marx's Workshop ("we," "our," or "us") operates as an independent creator store. This Privacy Policy outlines how we handle information when you visit our site or interact with our services.

1. Information Collection

We do not directly collect or store payment information on this static informational website. Purchases are processed externally via secure platforms such as Etsy or TikTok Shop.

2. Use of Third-Party Integrations

We use verified third-party services to power our visual discovery features:

  • Pinterest API: We integrate with Pinterest to showcase product inspiration. Interactions with Pinterest elements are governed by Pinterest's user privacy policies.
  • Marketplaces: Links to Etsy and TikTok Shop direct you to external secure platforms with their own strict privacy standards.

3. Data Usage

Any data retrieved via visual APIs (like Pinterest) is used strictly to facilitate visual search, catalog organization, and product presentation. We do not sell, rent, or distribute user data to outside marketers.

4. Your Rights

Depending on your region, you may have rights regarding the transparency of the platform. Please contact us directly via our shop interface for inquiries regarding your interaction with our product catalogs.

5. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us through our Etsy store or visual platform profiles.
